Featured thesis project

DARAS

Digital Addiction Risk Assessment System

Capstone projectAIRisk assessment

DARAS is based on my Master's thesis topic and was developed as the capstone project for the Tech Industry Readiness Program. The system explores how behavioural data can support earlier understanding of digital addiction risk.

Its direction brings together data collection, preprocessing, exploratory analysis, machine learning, responsible interpretation, and a practical user-facing system.

  • Problem: make digital addiction risk easier to notice and discuss.
  • Approach: use data science to organize signals into interpretable assessment output.
  • Outcome: a socially relevant project connecting academic research with industry-ready delivery.
